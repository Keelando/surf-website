🌊 Wave Forecast — Halibut Bank

Experimental Use at your own risk. Modelled waves from Environment Canada's regional wave model (RDWPS), updated four times a day.

About this model: RDWPS is Environment Canada's regional wave model. It works out the sea state on a 2.5 km grid and looks 48 hours ahead, re-running every six hours as new wind observations come in. These numbers are the model's raw output at the Halibut Bank buoy — nothing here is tuned to local knowledge, so treat it as a good read on wind-driven seas and a rough one where the Strait's currents and channels dominate. The buoy readings on the home page are the check: same spot, actually measured.
