Forecast Verification

About these models: RDWPS is Environment Canada's regional wave model. It works out the sea state on a 2.5 km grid and looks 48 hours ahead, re-running every six hours as new wind observations come in. The wind is a separate model — HRDPS, the 2.5 km atmospheric one — because RDWPS publishes its own forcing wind only inside the raw model files, and HRDPS is the better wind source anyway. Wind is the 10 m sustained speed; the gust points appear only at the hours the model actually diagnoses a gust, so gaps there mean "none forecast", not "no data". These numbers are raw model output at the selected point — nothing here is tuned to local knowledge, so treat it as a good read on wind-driven seas and a rough one where the Strait's currents and channels dominate. The readings on the home page are the check: same spot, actually measured.
